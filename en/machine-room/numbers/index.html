The numbers

What the edge saw. Counted every hour, not invented.

Four boards from the same machinery: a Cron Trigger in the thing’s Worker fetches figures from Cloudflare’s APIs every hour, stores them in D1 and Workers KV, and this page reads them through Workers Cache. No IP addresses are fetched or shown. Pick a board – the address follows, so you can share it.

DefencesCrawlersSpeedDenmark right now

Every day robots knock on tingsvad.dk with known attacks against software the site does not even run. Cloudflare’s defences stop them in the data centre nearest the attacker, before they reach the thing’s Workers. Here is what the defences have seen in the last 30 days: where the attacks came from, which rules fired and what they were looking for. No IP addresses are fetched or shown.

How it is counted

  1. Cron Trigger
  2. GraphQL Analytics API
  3. D1
  4. Workers KV
  5. Workers Cache
  6. Your browser

The source

Cloudflare’s GraphQL Analytics API for the tingsvad.dk zone: firewall events, all requests and verified bots. The token may only read analytics for this one zone.

Every hour

A Cron Trigger in the thing’s Worker asks for today and yesterday, one day at a time, so Cloudflare does not have to sample over a long period. The rows are kept in D1 for 95 days.

The overview

After each collection the Worker builds the two overviews and puts them in Workers KV. The page fetches them through Workers Cache, which is purged with a cache tag when there are new figures.

What is not kept

IP addresses are neither fetched nor stored. Paths are shown without query strings, long tokens are hidden and email addresses in user agents are removed. Figures over long periods may be sampled by Cloudflare and are therefore approximate.

Raw figures as JSON: /api/ting-ai/vaern. Country centres: world-countries (ODbL). Country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain).

Not every guest is human. Search engines index the pages, AI crawlers collect text for language models, and AI assistants fetch a page because a person asked them to. The thing has put special files out for them. Here is who came in the last 30 days, what they read and who only pretended to be a bot. Nobody is blocked, and no IP addresses are fetched or shown.

Lighthouse measures the pages in a laboratory. Here they are measured with real guests: how long until the largest content was on screen, how fast the page answered a click, and whether it jumped around on the way. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures it with a small script without cookies and without knowing the guest, and the thing fetches the figures every hour.

How it is counted

  1. Web Analytics beacon
  2. GraphQL Analytics API
  3. Cron Trigger
  4. Workers KV
  5. Workers Cache
  6. Your browser

The source

Cloudflare Web Analytics (Real User Monitoring) for tingsvad.dk, fetched from the GraphQL Analytics API at account level. The token may only read analytics.

Every hour

A Cron Trigger in the thing’s Worker fetches the last 7 days: page views, Core Web Vitals, load times and where the time goes. Each part is fetched separately, so one failure does not take the rest with it.

The limits

Good, needs improvement and poor follow Google’s limits: LCP 2.5 and 4 seconds, INP 200 and 500 milliseconds, CLS 0.1 and 0.25. The figures are the 75th percentile, as Google uses.

What is not measured

Guests who block the script or have no JavaScript are not counted. No IP addresses, cookies or fingerprints are stored.

Raw figures as JSON: /api/ting-ai/hastighed.

The defences and the crawlers show what happens at one small ford. Cloudflare sees traffic from all of Denmark and shares it openly in Radar. Here the country’s figures stand next to the thing’s: how much of the traffic comes from bots, which AI crawlers are most active, where the attacks come from, how many connections are already quantum-safe, and how fast the Danish internet is.

How it is counted

  1. Cron Trigger
  2. Radar API
  3. Workers KV
  4. Workers Cache
  5. Your browser

The source

Cloudflare Radar for Denmark (location=DK), fetched with the Radar API. Radar is based on traffic through Cloudflare’s network and shows shares, not counts. Data is CC BY-NC 4.0.

Every hour

A Cron Trigger in the thing’s Worker fetches sixteen Radar summaries and puts them together in Workers KV. Each part is fetched separately, so one failure does not take the rest with it.

The thing’s own figures

The comparisons with tingsvad.dk come from the defences and the crawlers: the verified bots that came by, and the countries the attacks on the thing came from.

What is not stored

Radar is aggregate figures for the whole country. No data about individual people, IP addresses or sites is fetched.

Raw figures as JSON: /api/ting-ai/danmark.
