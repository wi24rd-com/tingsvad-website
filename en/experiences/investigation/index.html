Case file 8 · The evidence

Examine the evidence

Four objects have been presented in the case over 1,046 years. None has been examined, since an examination would require a decision to examine. You may.

Exhibit A · The cowObject of the case
Ownership
Disputed. Ulf Blackcloak claims ownership. The cow claims nothing, but has, through counsel (Gudrun), registered an objection to the term "object". The objection is adjourned, so for the time being the cow is an object that protests.
Testimony
"Moo." (980). "Moo." (1572). No statement (2012, as it was possibly a cat).
Expert assessment
Red-and-white, c. 1,046 years, good condition. The vet finds the age "unusual" and recommends the cow not be subjected to further proceedings. The recommendation has been entered into the minutes.
Status
Presented. Not examined. Standing at the fourth stone.
Exhibit B · The shieldAsgeir's
Ownership
Asgeir Shieldbearer. Undisputed, as nobody else has wanted it.
Testimony
Asgeir: "Not relevant to the case." The axes: no statement. The dragon: "not relevant either", according to Asgeir.
Expert assessment
Limewood, iron fittings, carved dragon in Urnes style. The conservator notes the shield is in remarkably good condition for something that has leaned against a stone for 1,046 years, and that it appears to have been polished. Asgeir has declined to comment on the polishing.
Status
Presented. Not relevant. Polished.
Exhibit C · The stoolGranted to Toke
Ownership
The thing. Right of use: Toke the Younger, per § 8. The right has not been exercised, as the question of whether one may sit is adjourned.
Testimony
Toke: "I would like to sit down." (repeated 1,016 times, once a year since 1010). The stool: no statement, but it creaks when passed.
Expert assessment
Three-legged oak stool, never loaded. The cabinetmaker rates it "as new" and adds that it is the saddest thing he has seen.
Status
Presented. On the bank. Moved 4 cm (unexplained).
Exhibit D · The spearThe chair's
Ownership
Passed down to the chair, per § 5. Current holder: Chieftain Tingsvad (the First), who has not handed it over, as he has not stopped.
Testimony
The chieftain: "It is used for pointing." The spearhead: sharp. Ragnhild: "I have two axes."
Expert assessment
Ash shaft, iron head, never used for anything but pointing. The weapons historian notes it is the only Viking spear in the North still under full warranty.
Status
Presented. Pointing. Leaning against the tree.
Case file 8 · Supplement · The investigation
