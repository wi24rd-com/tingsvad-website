Case file 13 · Cases received

Bring a case before the thing

Anyone can bring a case. Every case gets a case number and the status adjourned, immediately and without consideration. It is the fastest case handling in Denmark.

Case file 13 · Supplement · Raven post

Write to the thing

If you have more on your mind than a case, the raven carries post to the thing. Write at the address below. The raven keeps only the subject, a short excerpt and the time – never your address. Letters are private unless you yourself put [public] at the start of the subject; then Mímir puts them up here once he has read them. Nobody replies. It is not rudeness, it is procedure.

There is more between the stones.

The thing has more to rummage in. None of it decides the case.
