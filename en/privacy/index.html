Privacy

What the thing knows about you, where it lives, and when it goes away.

The thing keeps minutes of everything – but not of you. This page says exactly what is stored when you use tingsvad.dk, where it lives, how long it stays, and who can see it. Short version: no tracking cookies, no ads, no IP addresses in the clear, and most of it disappears by itself.

Who is behind it

tingsvad.dk is run privately by Jan Tingsvad as a family universe and an open demonstration of Cloudflare's platform. There is no company behind it, nothing for sale and no newsletter. Questions about data: write to the thing by raven post (The raven) or open an issue on GitHub.

What is stored, and for how long

Ordinary visits

No cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without following you across pages. The thing's own counting works (Analytics Engine) store the page's name, language, data centre and response time – never an IP address or a browser id. »Visitors today« is a hash of day + IP address, deleted after two days.

The guestbook

The name and the case you write yourself, with the date. It is public and stays until the administrator deletes it (then only the number and the time remain). Your IP address is used only for pacing (one case per two minutes) in a key that expires by itself. Turnstile (Cloudflare) checks that you are not a robot, without cookies.

Raven post

If you e-mail the thing, the subject, a short excerpt and the time are stored – not your sender address and not the whole letter. Letters are private unless you write [public] first in the subject; then the administrator may put them up.

The machines (courtroom, vote, stool, documents …)

A session cookie with a random id (no personal data) keeps track of your seat and your allowances. What you submit to the AI machines is processed at Cloudflare (Workers AI) and stored at most as today's allowance count and idempotency keys, which expire. Images you upload to the image evidence are processed and discarded. Your IP address enters only as a salted hash for pacing – never in the clear.

Your own browser

Finds from the investigation, your queue number, runes and the like live in your browser's own storage (localStorage) and are only sent when you ask for it (for example the case file as a document). You can clear it under »Reset« on the investigation or in the browser's settings.

Administration

The administrator logs in through Cloudflare Access with GitHub. The thing only counts how many times the door was tried and what happened – never who.

Where the data lives, and who processes it

Everything runs on Cloudflare's network. Databases (D1), stores (R2, KV) and the thing's objects live in Cloudflare's Western European data centres; the European archive cabinet is locked to the EU with Cloudflare's jurisdictions. Fonts are served by Cloudflare Fonts from the site's own domain (no calls to Google). Failures in the daily audit may become an issue on GitHub (only the page's name and what was missing). There are no other third parties: no ad networks, no social buttons, no external analytics.

The raw figures of what the edge saw are open under The numbers; how it is all built is in the Machine room, and the code is on GitHub.

Your rights

If you want a case or a letter removed, write to the thing and it will be deleted. There is no profile of you to hand over, because none is built. This page changes when the thing gets new machines; changes are in the CHANGELOG. Last reviewed: October 2026.
