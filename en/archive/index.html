Case file 14 · The minutes

The minutes of the thing, from Jelling to today

The minutes have been kept by Mímir since 1636 and by Gudrun the goose before him. Since 2026 they are kept digitally by the chieftain himself, on a laptop carved from granite. The machine has one key. It is called adjourned. The battery lasts a thousand years, which the thing considers tight.

Below, the minutes are woven together with the events of the wider world, to the extent that the thing has noticed them. The birth of Denmark and the invention of the computer are both recorded. Neither has affected the case.

Denmark comes into beingComputers and AIThe thing
What we actually know

Every date about Denmark, computing and AI in the minutes is correct: Babbage's Analytical Engine (1837), Turing's undecidability proof (1936), ENIAC (1945), the Dartmouth workshop that named AI (1956), ARPANET's first message "LO" (1969), Berners-Lee's web (1991), Deep Blue vs. Kasparov (1997), Bluetooth named after Harald Bluetooth (1998), the AlexNet breakthrough (2012) and ChatGPT (2022). The thing's reactions are invented.

Case file 15 · The bylaws

The bylaws of Tingsvad

The rules are adopted. It is applying them that is adjourned.

  1. The thing meets at the ford when the fog permits. The fog rarely permits.
  2. Any case may be adjourned. Every case is.
  3. Dogs, wolves, geese and other animals have the right to speak, but not to vote. Mímir has objected. Gudrun has joined the objection. The objection is adjourned.
  4. Whoever brings a cow bears the burden of proof. Whoever brings two cows also bears the second cow.
  5. The spear belongs to the chair. The chair belongs to the family. The family belongs to the domain.
  6. The shield belongs to Asgeir. It is not relevant to the case. This is the only bylaw Asgeir himself has proposed.
  7. The item "any other business" is reached only when all preceding items have been decided. See § 2.
  8. Nobody may leave the thing while the thing is in session. The thing has been in session since 980. Toke the Younger has asked for a stool. The stool has been granted but not taken into use, as the case of whether one may sit down at the thing is adjourned.
  9. Women have the right to vote at the thing. The bylaw was passed unanimously on 5 June 1915, the same day the Danish constitution gave women the vote, after Ragnhild looked at the seven. It is the thing's second ruling and its fastest. Takes effect: when the item is reached.
  10. The network may only be used to send "adjourned". All other traffic is considered a ruling and is therefore forbidden.

Toke the Younger, § 8. The stool is on the bank. He has not sat down.

Case file 19 · Local facts

Tingsvad in numbers and fog

  • 1,046 yearsThe age of the case. It is thereby older than the Danish constitution, the University of Copenhagen and most cows.
  • 7 stonesThe stepping stones of the ford. The fourth is loose. Everybody knows. Nobody has done anything about it, as the case of the stone is adjourned.
  • 3 rulingsThe thing's total output: "We are standing here" (1572), women's right to vote (1915, after Ragnhild looked) and the domain (1990s). Since then: none. Legally the first counts as an observation.
  • 312 daysAverage number of foggy days a year at the ford. The remaining 53 are known locally as "summer".
  • 1 cowNumber of cows the case concerns. The cow's age is estimated at 1,046 years, which researchers find "unusual for a cow".
  • 2 minute-keepersGudrun (goose, 1236–1636 and 2026–) and Mímir (wolf, 1636–). Neither plans to retire, as that case would have to be heard by the thing.
  • 6 runestonesThe nodes of the network. One server, five clients, one of which is the backup. Total bandwidth: one word.
  • 1 spearPassed down to the chair. Has never been used for anything but pointing. For that reason the tip is still sharp.
  • 1 stoolGranted to Toke the Younger. Stands on the bank. Has never been sat on. The district's best-preserved piece of furniture.
  • 1 keyOn the chieftain's granite laptop. It is called "adjourned". No others are needed.
  • 4 cmHow far the stool has moved since 1010. By whom is unexplained. Toke denies it. The stool does not comment.
  • 1 committeeAppointed in 2026 to investigate how Ragnhild could decide the case. Its first meeting is adjourned.
Case file 22 · Frequently asked questions

Questions Mímir has heard before.

Answered by Mímir. The answers were entered into the minutes as one word, as usual, and then translated.

Where is Tingsvad?

In the fog. Follow the river until you can no longer see the river. That's it.

When did Denmark come into being?

The name is first mentioned around 890 and carved in stone at Jelling around 965. The thing was convened around 980 and therefore regards Denmark as an older, but less persistent, institution.

Can I have my case heard by the thing?

Yes. Cases are heard in the order received. There is one case ahead of you. It is from 980.

Who owns the cow?

That is precisely the question.

Why does Asgeir bring a shield?

He has never explained it, and the thing has decided not to ask, since an answer would require a ruling. See case file 3, which does not explain it either.

Is Mímir a dog or a wolf?

Mímir is a wolf. He is, however, willing to be a dog if it would get the case closed. The offer has been noted and adjourned.

What does Mímir's minute-book say?

One word. It is the same word as always. He keeps it next to him so he does not forget it.

Can I e-mail the thing?

The thing does not read e-mail. It has barely read the minutes. Cases are raised in the guestbook, where there is only one case ahead of you.

When is the next thing?

When the fog lifts. See the foggy days under Local facts in the Archive.

Case file 23 · Practical matters

How to find Tingsvad

  1. Find the river.Any river. The fog does the rest.
  2. Follow the river upstream.The network sends downstream, so you are walking against the messages. That is intentional.
  3. When you can no longer see the river, stop.You are at the ford. You cannot see it, but neither can the thing.
  4. Find the fourth stone.It is loose. That is how you know it is the right one.
  5. If you can see the river, you have gone too far.Turn around. So did the cow.

Case file 24 · Notes

None of the above is true, except for the meaning of ting and vad and the lines in the minutes about Denmark, computers and artificial intelligence – those dates are correct; only the thing's reaction is made up. Ting and vad really do mean what they say, and yes, this is how place names come about. The domain is also real and has been in the family since the 90s, and it is true that Frantz and Kamma took the name Tingsvad when they married in the 1940s. It is the only moment in this story where anyone decided anything, and it was not the thing. The cow, Mímir, Gudrun, Asgeir's shield, the network, the stool, the family tree, the dictionary, the accounts and the man who voted, on the other hand, we have never seen. The cases in the guestbook are real, in the sense that someone wrote them. They are also adjourned. Source 1 has not survived, and strictly speaking neither has the quote. Source 2, on the other hand, is real: the keys, inheritance rights, divorce rules, Thyra on the Jelling Stone and warrior grave Bj 581 at Birka are history, not humour. Ragnhild is invented. Her point is not.
